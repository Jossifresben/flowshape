## Formula

    x(t) = cos(n₁t + φ₁),  y(t) = cos(n₂t + φ₂),  z(t) = cos(n₃t + φ₃),   t ∈ [0, 2π]
    with (n₁, n₂, n₃) pairwise coprime integers,
    and phases kept clear of the singular set  nᵢφⱼ − nⱼφᵢ ≡ 0 (mod π)

## What it means

Take the familiar Lissajous figure — a cosine on each axis — and give it a third axis. The point now traces a curve through space, and two small theorems do all the ordering. Because the three frequencies are whole numbers, the curve closes after exactly one period: it cannot help returning to its start. And because they are pairwise coprime — no two share a factor — it is a genuine space curve rather than a multiple cover of a shorter one. Bogle, Hearst, Jones and Stoilov studied exactly these curves in 1994 and showed that many of them are knots in the strict sense: closed loops that cannot be untangled without cutting.

The phases are where the freedom lives. They are drawn from the seed, but not blindly: whenever a pair of phases lands near the singular set — the values where the projection folds over onto a doubly-traced arc — it is redrawn, so every seed yields a genuine flourish and none a scribble.

What you see is that space curve projected onto the page, and the projection is this project's own construction: the curve is cut into depth bands, and nearer strands are drawn wider and more opaque than farther ones, so the over-and-under of the knot stays legible as a made object. In motion the whole figure tumbles by exactly one turn per cycle, its three axes breathe at integer rates, and a trail of layers fans open mid-cycle and closes at the wrap — every motion an identity at phase 1, so the loop is seamless by arithmetic, not by editing.

## Parameters

**Frequencies** picks the coprime triple (n₁, n₂, n₃) — higher triples wind more times before closing. **Tilt** tips the projection so depth becomes visible. **Breathe** lets the three axis weights swell at different integer beats. **Depth** is the strength of the near/far contrast — at zero the projection is flat, at full the far strands almost vanish. **Layers** overlays the curve a little further along its own motion, an engraved trail. The seed rolls new phases — a new knot from the same triple.
